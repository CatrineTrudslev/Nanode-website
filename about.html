About naNODE

What is naNODE?

naNODE is an alumni association for current students and graduates of the Nanoscience programme at the University of Copenhagen. We connect people across different stages of their studies and careers.

Our mission

Our mission is to build a lasting nanoscience community through social and professional events, shared knowledge and meaningful connections between students and alumni.

Meet the Board

The naNODE board plans the association's activities, develops new initiatives and works to keep our community active and welcoming.
