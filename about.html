ABOUT NANODE

Connecting Copenhagen's nanoscience community

naNODE is an alumni association for students and graduates of the Nanoscience programme at the University of Copenhagen.

Our purpose

We bring students and alumni together across different stages of the nanoscience career path. The association creates a lasting connection between the study programme and the community that grows from it.

What we do

Through social and professional events, we create opportunities to meet, exchange ideas and build strong networks across generations.

Our community

naNODE is for current students and graduates of the Nanoscience programme, including bachelor's, master's and PhD alumni.
